Know Our Village

A beautiful village with culture, unity and progress.

Our Village, Our Pride

Gankhaira is a beautiful village known for its agriculture, paddy cultivation and several rice mills. Farming is an important part of village life, where farmers grow paddy and produce rice, contributing to the local economy.
